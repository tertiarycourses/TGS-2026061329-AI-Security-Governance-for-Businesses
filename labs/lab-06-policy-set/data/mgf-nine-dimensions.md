# Singapore Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI — The Nine Dimensions
IMDA / AI Verify Foundation. Use this as the mapping reference for your policy set.

| # | Dimension | What it asks of an organisation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Accountability** | Allocate responsibility across the AI development chain, so end-users have someone answerable. Parallels exist with cloud and software stacks. |
| 2 | **Data** | Ensure data quality and trusted sources. Give clarity and fair treatment where the data is contentious — personal data and copyright material especially. |
| 3 | **Trusted Development and Deployment** | Adopt best practice in development and evaluation, and provide "food label"-type transparency on the baseline safety and hygiene measures taken. |
| 4 | **Incident Reporting** | Establish structures and processes for incident monitoring, timely notification and remediation, feeding continuous improvement. |
| 5 | **Testing and Assurance** | Use third-party testing and assurance for independent verification, and support common standards so testing is consistent and comparable. |
| 6 | **Security** | Recognise that GenAI introduces new threat vectors through the models themselves; adapt existing information-security frameworks and develop new testing tools. |
| 7 | **Content Provenance** | Support transparency about where and how content was generated — digital watermarking and cryptographic provenance, used in the right context. |
| 8 | **Safety and Alignment R&D** | Invest in improving model alignment with human intention and values; cooperate globally across AI safety institutes. |
| 9 | **AI for Public Good** | Steer AI development towards broad benefit — democratised access, public-sector adoption, upskilling and sustainability. |

**AI Verify** is Singapore's AI governance testing framework and toolkit: it lets an organisation
demonstrate through standardised tests that its system behaves as claimed, rather than only
asserting it. Testing produces the evidence that dimensions 3 and 5 call for.
