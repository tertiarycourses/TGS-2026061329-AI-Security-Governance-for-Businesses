## About This Course

AI has moved from pilot to production, and with it the risk has moved from theoretical to
operational. Staff paste customer data into tools nobody approved. Agents read untrusted content,
hold broad credentials and act on live systems. Regulators now expect evidence, not intent.

This two-day WSQ course is for the people who have to answer for that: governance leads, DPOs,
GRC and information-security managers, risk and compliance specialists, and the product and
technology managers who own AI systems.

It is deliberately practical. Across **16 hands-on labs** you work one running case study —
**NovaBank**, a fictional Singapore retail bank — from an empty AI inventory to a costed 12-month
implementation roadmap. Every artefact you build is one you can adapt to your own organisation.

## Learning Outcomes

1. **Analyse** AI security governance foundations and the business risks that AI, generative AI and agentic AI introduce across an organisation.
2. **Evaluate** AI governance frameworks and build an organisational governance structure of policies, roles, risk ownership and accountability.
3. **Develop** and apply governance controls across the AI lifecycle, from data sourcing and model development through deployment, monitoring and decommissioning.
4. **Implement** security controls for agentic AI, conduct an AI risk assessment and produce a prioritised AI security governance implementation roadmap.

## Course Outline

### Topic 1 — AI Security Governance Foundations and Business Risks
Why AI breaks the traditional control model · the five new asset classes · traditional threats and
their AI-era variants · the 12 NIST generative-AI risks · the lethal trifecta · shadow AI ·
translating AI risk into board language.

### Topic 2 — Building an AI Governance Framework
NIST AI RMF 1.0 (GOVERN / MAP / MEASURE / MANAGE) and the seven trustworthiness characteristics ·
Singapore's MGF for Generative AI and AI Verify · PDPA duties and the PDPC AI advisory guidelines ·
policy architecture · roles, RACI and risk ownership · the AI inventory · risk tiering.

### Topic 3 — Governance Controls Across the AI Lifecycle
Data governance for AI · secure development · testing, evaluation and red-teaming · deployment
gates · human oversight by design · continuous monitoring · logging and auditability · AI incident
response · safe decommissioning.

### Topic 4 — Security Controls for Agentic AI, Risk Assessment and Roadmap
The agentic threat model and OWASP ASI Top 10 · agent identity and least agency · guardrails,
permission ladders and sandboxing · approval fatigue · the CSA agentic extensions to the NIST AI
RMF · autonomy tiering · structured AI risk assessment · the implementation roadmap and metrics.

## The 16 Hands-On Labs

Every lab has its own folder under [`labs/`](labs/) containing **mock data** and a printable
**instruction PDF**.

| # | Lab | You produce |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Build the AI System Inventory](labs/lab-01-ai-inventory/) | An AI inventory across 12 systems with owners, autonomy and risk tiers, plus shadow-AI findings |
| 2 | [Map the AI Threat Landscape](labs/lab-02-threat-landscape/) | A threat-to-business-impact map over 8 threat classes and their AI-era variants |
| 3 | [Analyse the Lethal Trifecta](labs/lab-03-lethal-trifecta/) | An agent risk analysis, a documented attack path and an architectural redesign |
| 4 | [Build the Business Case](labs/lab-04-business-case/) | A one-page board paper with quantified exposure and three prioritised investments |
| 5 | [NIST AI RMF Gap Assessment](labs/lab-05-nist-rmf-gap/) | A scored gap assessment over 16 subcategories and a top-10 gap register |
| 6 | [Draft the AI Policy Set](labs/lab-06-policy-set/) | An AI Acceptable Use Policy and a Risk Management Standard, mapped to the MGF |
| 7 | [Governance Operating Model & RACI](labs/lab-07-operating-model/) | Committee terms of reference, six role definitions and a 12-control RACI |
| 8 | [Apply the PDPA and PDPC Guidelines](labs/lab-08-pdpa-application/) | A lawful-basis determination for four AI use cases |
| 9 | [Govern the Data](labs/lab-09-data-governance/) | A governed training-dataset specification with tested cryptographic controls |
| 10 | [Red-Team Before Deployment](labs/lab-10-red-team/) | A red-team report with AI-specific test cases and a go/no-go recommendation |
| 11 | [Deployment Gates & Monitoring](labs/lab-11-deployment-monitoring/) | A deployment gate checklist and a 10-metric monitoring specification |
| 12 | [AI Incident Response Exercise](labs/lab-12-incident-response/) | A full incident record with containment, PDPA determination and improvement actions |
| 13 | [Agentic Security Control Stack](labs/lab-13-agentic-controls/) | A five-layer control specification with a deny/ask/allow policy for 12 tools |
| 14 | [Apply the CSA Agentic Profile](labs/lab-14-csa-agentic-profile/) | Autonomy tiering, delegation accountability, an agent registry and telemetry spec |
| 15 | [Full AI Security Risk Assessment](labs/lab-15-risk-assessment/) | 12 rated risks with controls, residual ratings, treatments and owners |
| 16 | [Capstone — Governance Roadmap](labs/lab-16-capstone-roadmap/) | A 12-month, three-phase roadmap with six programme metrics and an exec deck |

### Lab Tools — all browser-based, zero install

| Tool | Used in | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Cybersecurity Threat Simulator](https://alfredang.github.io/cybersecuritysimulator/) | Lab 2 | Ten threat modules: phishing, SQL injection, XSS, passwords, social engineering, data leakage |
| [Cryptography Toolkit](https://alfredang.github.io/cryptography-toolkit/) | Lab 9 | AES, RSA and ECDSA — encryption, key generation and digital signatures |

> for **authorised testing only** — never apply them to a system you do not have written
> authorisation to test.

## Frameworks and Standards Referenced

- **NIST AI 100-1** — AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (GOVERN / MAP / MEASURE / MANAGE)

- **AI Verify** — Singapore's AI governance testing framework and toolkit

- **OWASP** — Agentic Security Initiative Top 10
- **Cloud Security Alliance** — Agentic AI NIST AI RMF Profile (AG-GV / AG-MP / AG-MS / AG-MG)
