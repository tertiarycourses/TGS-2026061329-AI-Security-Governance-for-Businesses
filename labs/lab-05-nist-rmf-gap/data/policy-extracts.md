# NovaBank — Policy Extracts (evidence pack for RMF assessment)

## Extract A — Information Security Policy (v6.2, last reviewed Mar 2026)
> 3.1 All information systems shall be registered in the Configuration Management Database (CMDB)
> prior to production deployment.
> 3.4 System owners are accountable for the security posture of their systems.
> 7.2 All systems processing Confidential data shall undergo a security review before go-live.
> **Note:** the policy makes no reference to AI, machine learning or models. "System" is defined
> as "an application or platform deployed on NovaBank infrastructure" — which the Digital Channels
> team has argued does not cover SaaS AI features.

## Extract B — Data Protection Policy (v3.1, last reviewed Nov 2025)
> 4.1 Personal data shall be collected and used only for purposes notified to the individual or
> permitted under the PDPA.
> 4.6 The Data Protection Officer shall maintain a record of processing activities.
> **Note:** the record of processing activities has not been updated since Jan 2025 and does not
> include any AI system.

## Extract C — Third Party Risk Management Standard (v2.4)
> 5.2 Vendors handling Confidential data shall complete the Vendor Security Assessment
> Questionnaire annually.
> **Note:** the questionnaire contains no AI-specific questions — no model provenance, no training
> data questions, no sub-processor AI disclosure.

## Extract D — Change Management Procedure (v4.0)
> 6.1 All production changes require CAB approval.
> **Note:** model retraining and prompt changes are handled by the Digital Channels team as
> "configuration", and do not go to CAB.

## Extract E — AI-related policy
> None. An "AI Usage Guidance" one-pager was circulated by e-mail in Feb 2026 by the CTO's office.
> It is not a policy of record, has no owner, no version and no attestation.
