# NovaBank — Four PDPA Scenarios for Determination

## Scenario 1 — Product Recommendation Model
NovaBank wants to use existing retail customers' transaction history, product holdings and
demographic data to train a model that recommends additional products (a savings plan, a card
upgrade) in the mobile app. The data was collected when customers opened their accounts, under
notification for "providing banking services". No fresh consent has been obtained for model
training. Marketing wants to launch in six weeks.
**Determine:** lawful basis, whether an exception applies, notification required, controls.

## Scenario 2 — Credit Decision Support Model
The credit decision engine scores retail loan applications. The score materially influences
approve/decline. Declined applicants sometimes ask why. Currently the adverse-decision letter says
"your application did not meet our criteria". The model uses income, employment tenure, existing
obligations, repayment history and postal code.
**Determine:** lawful basis, notification, explainability requirement, human oversight measure,
and the risk arising from the postal-code feature.

## Scenario 3 — Staff-Facing GenAI Assistant
NovaBank is rolling out M365 Copilot to 900 staff. Copilot can read any file the individual user
can access. Staff have already been observed pasting customer names, account numbers and
correspondence into public GenAI tools (see the Lab 1 staff survey). Legal wants to know what must
be in place before the rollout.
**Determine:** the PDPA risks, the controls required, the retention position, and what must be told
to staff and to customers.

## Scenario 4 — Third-Party Developed Fraud Model
NovaBank engages an external vendor, Sentinel Analytics, to build and host a bespoke fraud
detection model. NovaBank sends the vendor five years of transaction data including customer names,
NRIC, account numbers and transaction detail. The vendor will use the data to build NovaBank's
model, and has asked whether it may also use "anonymised learnings" to improve its other clients'
models.
**Determine:** the vendor's status under the PDPA, the obligations that follow, the contractual
terms NovaBank requires, and how to answer the "anonymised learnings" request.
