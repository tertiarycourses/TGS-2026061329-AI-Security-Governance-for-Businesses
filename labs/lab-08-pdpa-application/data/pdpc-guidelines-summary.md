# PDPC Advisory Guidelines on the Use of Personal Data in AI Recommendation and Decision Systems
Issued 1 March 2024. Not legally binding, but the PDPC will take consistent positions in enforcing
the PDPA. Reference summary for Lab 8.

## Structure
- **Part III — Development, testing and monitoring:** when the Business Improvement Exception and
  the Research Exception let an organisation use personal data *without consent*.
- **Part IV — Deployment:** the Consent and Notification Obligations and the Accountability
  Obligation as they apply to personal data collected and used in a live AI system.
- **Part V — Procurement:** how Service Providers (third-party developers) support organisations.

## The two exceptions
**Business Improvement Exception** — relevant where the organisation is improving or developing
an existing product or service, e.g. an AI system providing personalised product recommendations.
It also caters for sharing within a group of related companies. The guidelines note it can apply
to the use of personal data for bias assessment and for testing the AI system.

**Research Exception** — relevant where the organisation is conducting broader commercial research
to develop AI systems with public benefit (the guidelines give precision medicine as an example),
and covers disclosure of personal data for a research purpose subject to conditions.

## Deployment obligations
- **Consent and Notification** — where consent is relied on, notification must be meaningful.
  Organisations should be able to tell individuals what personal data is used and, broadly, how the
  AI system uses it to make its recommendation or decision.
- **Accountability** — organisations must be able to show *how* they discharge their obligations:
  policies and practices, the basis for relying on an exception recorded in writing, measures adopted
  to protect individuals' interests, and disclosure practices such as model cards and system cards.

## Data protection measures expected when using personal data in AI development
Minimisation · anonymisation or pseudonymisation where feasible · access control · retention limits ·
technical and process safeguards · documentation of the decision and its basis.

## Service Providers
Third-party developers of bespoke AI systems are **data intermediaries** and carry the
**Protection** and **Retention** Obligations under the PDPA.
