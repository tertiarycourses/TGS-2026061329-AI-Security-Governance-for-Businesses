# Executive Presentation — Five Slides, Five Minutes

## Slide 1 — Where we are
The AI estate in one picture: how many systems, how many High-risk, how many shadow AI,
current maturity per domain. No jargon.

## Slide 2 — What could go wrong
Your top three rated risks from Lab 15, as consequences in business language.
Include the one incident scenario you can describe concretely (the NovaAssist injection).

## Slide 3 — What we will do
The three phases on one timeline. Initiatives grouped, not listed individually.
Show the dependency that drives the sequence.

## Slide 4 — What it costs
Effort and spend by phase. Set it against the exposure figure from Lab 4.

## Slide 5 — What you are approving
ONE clear ask. Budget, headcount, mandate — state it in a single sentence.
The decision you want, and the date you need it by.

---
**Delivery:** five minutes. Expect one challenge from the board chair. The most common are:
- "Why can't we just buy a tool for this?"
- "What happens if we do nothing for six months?"
- "Is this not just the CISO's job already?"
Prepare a one-sentence answer to each.
