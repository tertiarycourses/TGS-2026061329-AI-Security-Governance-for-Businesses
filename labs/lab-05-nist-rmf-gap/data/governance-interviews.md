# NovaBank — Governance Interview Notes (evidence pack)

## Interview 1 — Chief Risk Officer
"AI risk sits with the technology team at the moment. It hasn't come to the Risk Committee as a
standing item. I'd struggle to tell you how many AI systems we run — I'd guess three or four."
Asked about executive accountability for AI: "Ultimately me, I suppose, but it's not written down."

## Interview 2 — Head of Digital Channels
"We move fast. NovaAssist went from idea to pilot in nine weeks. We did a security review of the
infrastructure. We didn't do anything specific about the model — I'm not sure what that would even
look like." Asked about testing: "We tested that it answers correctly. We ran about 200 test
questions." Asked about bias or jailbreak testing: "No."

## Interview 3 — Data Protection Officer
"I found out about NovaAssist from a demo at town hall. I've asked for a DPIA-equivalent
assessment but there's no process that requires one, so it's a request rather than a gate."
Asked about the PDPC AI advisory guidelines: "I've read them. We haven't mapped our systems to them."

## Interview 4 — Head of IT Security
"We monitor infrastructure and endpoints well. We have no visibility into what prompts go to the
models, what tools the agents call, or what comes back. The Azure OpenAI logs exist but nobody owns
reviewing them." Asked about incident response: "The IR plan doesn't mention AI. If NovaAssist
started leaking data on a Saturday I genuinely don't know who would switch it off, or how fast."

## Interview 5 — Head of Credit Risk
"The credit model is validated annually by Model Risk Management — that's a mature process, it
predates all this. It's documented, back-tested and challenged." Asked whether the same applies to
GenAI systems: "No, MRM only covers regulatory capital and credit models."

## Interview 6 — Head of Internal Audit
"AI isn't on the audit plan for this year. We don't currently have the skills in the team to audit
a model. We'd rely on an external firm."

## Interview 7 — Marketing Manager
"We bought Jasper on the department card. IT wasn't involved — it's a SaaS writing tool, we didn't
think it needed a review." Asked what data goes in: "Product info, some customer segment data."
