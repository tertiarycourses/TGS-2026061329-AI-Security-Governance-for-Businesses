# AI Security Governance Maturity Model

| Level | Name | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Initial** | No AI-specific governance. Individual teams decide for themselves. Shadow AI unmeasured. |
| 2 | **Developing** | Policy exists. An inventory exists but is incomplete. Governance is a person, not a process. |
| 3 | **Defined** | Documented policy set, risk tiering, deployment gates and named owners. Applied consistently to new systems. |
| 4 | **Managed** | Controls measured. Monitoring with thresholds. Risk assessments refreshed on a cycle. Incidents produce improvement actions. |
| 5 | **Optimised** | Assurance is independent and routine. Metrics reported to the board. Controls automated and continuously tested. |
